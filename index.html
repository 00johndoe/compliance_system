Research Objectives

  • Compare Ghana's cited legal and directive requirements with ISO/IEC 27002 through control links, to identify overlaps and gaps.
  • Develop a compliance measurement model and automated web-based system that evaluates organizational alignment with both frameworks.
  • Test, validate, and generate compliance reports with actionable recommendations for improving cybersecurity alignment.

Scoring Methodology

Compliance Score = (Σ control_score) / (number of controls)

Each control is assessed on a 0-5 Capability Maturity Model scale, converted to a percentage, then averaged across all controls to produce a 0-100% score.

Glossary
Ghana requirements
This project's plain-language summary of duties in the Cybersecurity Act 2020 (Act 1038), the Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843) and the CSA Directive for Critical Information Infrastructure, each with its citation. It is not an official CSA publication.
CII
Critical Information Infrastructure: systems the CSA has formally designated as critical. The CII Directive applies only to their owners.
ISO/IEC 27002:2022
An international standard of information security controls and guidance.
Control
A safeguard or measure an organization puts in place to manage a security risk.
Control mapping
Linking each Ghana requirement to the ISO 27002 control(s) that address a similar need. The links are proposals that have not yet been validated.
CMM
Capability Maturity Model, the 0–5 scale used here to rate how mature a control is.
Gap analysis
Finding requirements with no ISO control linked to them, and ISO controls that no Ghana requirement refers to.
