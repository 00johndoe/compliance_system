How to use this

1. Treat the law as the starting point

The Ghana requirements are legal duties that apply according to what your organization does. ISO/IEC 27002 helps you meet them but does not replace them.

2. Use ISO for the rest through a risk assessment

Controls that no requirement refers to are optional good practice. Select those that address risks you have actually identified.

3. Have the links reviewed before relying on them

The links are proposals. Ask your supervisor, and for production use an ISO 27001 Lead Auditor in Ghana, to validate them.
